Force Panel Book

Every plugin on the Force, redrawn after the machine it models: the same sections in the same order, the knobs where the hardware has them. Under each page are its Q-Links in Screen mode: the Force's eight knobs left to right, then the second bank. A page with more than 16 controls has several Q-Link sets (A, B, C), which MPC lists as sub-pages of that page's tab.
